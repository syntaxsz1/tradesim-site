TradeSim — Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

TradeSim is a Discord bot that runs a simulated stock, crypto and forex trading game with real market prices and virtual money. This policy explains what data the bot stores and why.

What we collect

Discord identity. Your Discord user ID and username, used to attach your game account to you and to show your name on leaderboards.

Gameplay data. Your virtual balance, holdings, orders, forex / options / perpetual / short positions, watchlist, price alerts, firm membership, duel records, copy-trading settings, season statistics and daily portfolio snapshots used to draw your equity chart.

Server configuration. The server and channel IDs a server admin picks with /setup-trade, so trade alerts and the trade tape post in the right channel.

What we do NOT collect

TradeSim does not read or store your message content — the bot does not use Discord's Message Content privileged intent. We never collect passwords, email addresses, payment information (TradeSim has no payments), or any real banking, brokerage or financial account data.

How your data is used

Only to run the game: keeping your portfolio, filling orders, settling duels and seasons, sending you the in-game notifications you trigger (order fills, liquidations, expiries, price alerts, duel results), and showing leaderboards. Leaderboards publicly display Discord usernames and in-game statistics inside Discord — that is a core part of the game.

Market data providers

Prices come from public third-party feeds (CoinGecko, Yahoo Finance and Finnhub). Those providers receive only anonymous price requests from the bot — never your Discord identity or gameplay data.

Sharing

We never sell your data and never share it with advertisers.

Storage, retention and deletion

Your data is stored in the bot's database while your account exists. You can wipe your portfolio at any time with the in-game Reset button. To have your account and all related records fully deleted, contact us at the address below and we will delete them within 30 days. Removing the bot from a server does not by itself delete your global game account.

Direct messages

The bot sends DMs only for game events tied to your own activity (fills, liquidations, alerts, duel outcomes). You can stop them at any time by disabling direct messages from server members in your Discord settings.

Children

TradeSim is available wherever Discord is. You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement for your country to use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
